BOTTOM LINE
  Two independent checks agree the unconditional disorder premium collapses; a
  CONTROLLED one-variable-at-a-time decomposition (DIAG_Q1Q3.md / DIAG_channels.md)
  shows the collapse has TWO drivers, not one:
   * The unconditional "disorder is rewarded" / ΔG sign-inversion premium is
     driven by (i) UNIVERSE BREADTH — it is a large-cap S&P500 phenomenon that
     does not generalize to the full US-common universe (~60% of the FM t(ΔS)
     collapse), and (ii) SURVIVORSHIP among the mostly small-cap delisted tail
     (~30%), which removes the remainder and pushes t through zero. Frequency is
     immaterial (~8%). So the vanishing (R18 FM t=+0.02) / reversal (R19) is
     survivorship + non-generalization, NOT a pure survivorship artifact.
     (FM t(ΔS) ladder: +4.59 monthly-S&P500 -> +4.17 quarterly -> +1.28 full-
     universe survivor -> -0.28 full-universe incl delisted.)
   * The two STRUCTURAL results survive AND are shown robust by the same
     decomposition: (a) the T·ΔS Wald test (p=0.013) and (b) the asymmetric
     temperature prediction (β_ΔS~T: t=+3.11 S&P500 -> +3.64 corrected; breadth
     strengthens it, survivorship neutral). The stability channel ΔH is robustly
     positively priced at every rung (FM t: +2.70 S&P500 -> +3.46 corrected).
     NB: the asymmetric-prediction estimator survives, but the FM cross-sectional
     T·ΔS LEVEL coefficient collapses like ΔS (+4.59 -> -0.55) — the same
     FM-vs-Wald estimator disagreement noted below and in the 6/17 handoff.
   * Separation (Corr ΔH,ΔS=-0.28) and ΔH coverage (93%) are stable.
