Caveat, stated per the method's own limitation: this design cannot produce a
'contradicted' verdict -- it only distinguishes confirmed-by-co-occurrence from
not-found. A successor link that IS real but involves a name change, a shell
merger with minimal SEC paper trail, or predates full-text-searchable EDGAR
(pre-2001) would show as indeterminate or not-checkable here, not as evidence
against the link. This bounds the CONFIRMED rate as a lower bound on link
validity, not a point estimate of the true validation rate.

DIAGNOSED CAUSE of the 57% not-checkable rate (verified, not a lookup bug):
53 of the 57 not-checkable cases (93%) are "missing name/date", and of those,
53/53 trace to the SAME root cause -- the successor ticker in `relatedtickers`
has NO entry with a resolvable company name in ANY of Sharadar's four ticker
tables (SF1, SEP, SF3B, SFP combined), not just the SF1 table originally
queried. Checked directly: of this sample's 100 successor tickers, 22 are
found in SF1, 32 in SEP, 15 in SFP, 0 in SF3B, and 53 in NONE of the four.
This is a genuine data-quality finding about the `relatedtickers` field
itself -- roughly half of its links point to symbols (warrants/units,
OTC/pink-sheet issues, or tickers Sharadar references but never onboarded
as a full master record) that cannot be name-resolved from Sharadar's own
data at all, independent of anything EDGAR-side. Section 3.1's citation of
51.4% relatedtickers coverage should note that "populated" does not imply
"independently resolvable" -- roughly half of populated links point to a
symbol this same data provider does not otherwise carry a name for.
