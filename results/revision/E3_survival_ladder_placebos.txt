VERDICT: survival conditioning inflates MULTIPLE placebo slopes in magnitude too,
not just DeltaS -- Size (|t| 0.13->2.54) and Beta (|t| 0.39->1.81) both show moves
comparable in size to DeltaS's own (|t| 0.28->3.33), and Momentum (|t| 0.05->1.39)
shows a smaller but real move in the same direction. R20 demonstrates a property of
survival-conditioned panels more broadly (shrinking, longer-surviving samples
systematically move MULTIPLE unrelated cross-sectional slopes toward larger
|t|-statistics, not just DeltaS) rather than something specific to disorder/entropy.
The exhibit survives as a description of the DeltaS collapse-and-recovery pattern,
but the causal interpretation needs to narrow from 'survival conditioning
manufactures THE premium' (singular, disorder-specific) to 'survival conditioning
manufactures premiums' (plural, a property of the conditioning, not the
characteristic) -- exactly the restatement the spec anticipated as the less
favorable but real possible outcome. Book-to-market is the partial exception
(already borderline-significant at k=0, t=+1.07, and moves only modestly further).
