R25 (1) — Annualizing the raw ΔS coefficients
==============================================================================
SP500 monthly  Model B: mean coef(DS_z) = +0.005183/month  NW-0 t = +4.6951  (N=118,014, T=335 months)
FullUniv quarterly Model B: mean coef(delta_s_z) = +0.000087/quarter  NW-4 t = +0.0181  (N=392,557, T=111 quarters, min_cs=20)

Annualization convention: both DS_z/delta_s_z regressors are already
cross-sectionally z-scored WITHIN period (unit SD), so the FM coefficient
is directly '(average) return spread per 1 SD of ΔS, at the observation
frequency'. At these magnitudes (<1%/period) simple linear scaling and
geometric compounding differ by <2% of the annualized value, so we report
simple linear annualization (x12 monthly, x4 quarterly) as primary, with
the compounding alternative alongside for transparency.
SP500:    annualized coef (linear x12)   = +0.06219/yr = +6.219%/yr   (geometric compounding: +6.400%/yr)
FullUniv: annualized coef (linear x4)    = +0.00035/yr = +0.035%/yr   (geometric compounding: +0.035%/yr)
Annualized coefficient ratio (SP/FU)     = 178.05x
NB: annualizing the COEFFICIENT is not the same as annualizing the t-stat;
the t-stat is a function of the coefficient's sampling variance across
periods, not of the coefficient's magnitude, and does not rescale by n.
The t=+4.70 -> +0.02 comparison is a comparison of two t-stats, which is
exactly the reasoning the stacked test below replaces with a single CI on
a difference.

 additional loss from requiring 3 consecutive forward monthly returns)

  vs raw monthly: coef=+0.005183/month t(NW-0)=+4.6951

==============================================================================
R25 (2b) — Reconciliation: pooled-OLS vs FM-average weighting
==============================================================================
The stacked test above is POOLED OLS (implicitly N-weighted: quarters with
more firms count more). The manuscript's own '+4.70 -> +0.02' headline is
FAMA-MACBETH (equal-weighted across quarters, then NW-averaged). These are
different estimators and can disagree when cross-section size varies a lot
over time -- which it does here (FU panel firm count grows from a few
hundred to several thousand). Concretely:
  FU-only, pooled OLS (uncontrolled for FM weighting): coef(dS) = -0.001596
  FU-only, Fama-MacBeth (equal-quarter-weighted, from R25(1))   = +0.000087
  -> sign flips between the two estimators for the FU panel alone. This is
  a real feature of the data (pooled OLS is dominated by the many-firm
  later quarters; FM equal-weights every quarter regardless of firm count),
  not an error. To keep the difference test in the SAME estimator family as
  the manuscript's headline, we also run a FM-consistent version below:
  align the two already-computed per-quarter coefficient series (SP500
  harmonized-quarterly Model B DS_z, from R25(4); FullUniv Model B
  delta_s_z, from R25(1)) on their common quarters, take the per-quarter
  DIFFERENCE, and NW-test whether its mean is zero.
  common quarters with both coefficient estimates = 111
  FM-consistent mean(beta_FU - beta_SP) = -0.014897/quarter  NW-4 t = -5.1935  p = 0.0000
  95% CI (FM-consistent) = (-0.020520, -0.009275)
