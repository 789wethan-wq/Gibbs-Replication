Interpretation: if the left-truncation-only t(IVOL) values above already
recover most of the movement from +0.02 to +3.23 WITHOUT requiring survival to
the end of the sample, the ladder's resurrection is at least partly a birth-cohort
(old/large-at-listing) artifact rather than a survivorship effect specifically.
If the left-truncation-only cohorts stay close to the unconditional +0.02 and the
full jump to +3.23 requires the additional 108-quarter run-length (survival)
restriction, survival conditioning is doing the separable work the paper claims.
