Note on methodology (self-caught, reported for transparency): an earlier pass of
this script restricted only by TICKER membership in the R18 panel (not by the exact
(ticker,quarter) OBSERVATION pairs that actually appear in it), giving N=865,063 --
nearly double the panel's real row count (434,016) -- and a median of 2 filings /
47% at n_distinct=1, matching R21's own UNRESTRICTED full-14,600-ticker output but
NOT matching what the R18 panel's actual observations look like. Restricting to the
exact (ticker,quarter) pairs in merged_sf1_quarterly_survfree.parquet (N=404,387,
93% of the panel's 434,016 rows -- the gap is quarters where GPM has a valid rolling
estimate but the return/DeltaS side of that observation does not) gives the numbers
above, which DO match the manuscript's cited ~17% at n_distinct=1 closely (17.0%).

FINDING: the median corrected-panel observation rests on 6 distinct
filings -- essentially the SAME as the monthly SP500 panel's median (6).
DeltaH is NOT a coarse instrument for most of the corrected panel; the coarseness is
concentrated in the ~17-18% mass-point tail the manuscript's existing mass-point
analysis already covers. Section 3.2's framing through the mass-point lens is
adequate as-is for the median/typical observation; no broader coarseness caveat is
needed beyond what's already there. (Q1=3 filings is worth noting alongside the
mass-point figure, since a quarter of observations sit at 3 or fewer.)
