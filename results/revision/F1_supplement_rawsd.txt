========================================================================================
F1 supplement — raw return-SD proxy (broader coverage than the FF3 proxy)
========================================================================================
The 4-quarter FF3-residual proxy is INFEASIBLE, not just unreported: a 4-
parameter regression (const + Mkt_RF + SMB + HML) on exactly 4 observations
has zero residual degrees of freedom, so no residual variance is defined.
This is a mathematical fact, not a data-availability gap -- reported as such
rather than substituting a nearby window that happens to run.
