SUMMARY
============================================================================================
1. Enron, WorldCom, Lehman Brothers: ALL THREE present in SF1 (ENRNQ, WCOEQ, LEHMQ) with full quarterly fundamentals+price coverage through their actual final filing dates, and ALL THREE already included in the manuscript's own R18 survivorship-free analysis panel. The exact ticker strings Section 3.1 queried (ENE, ENRN, WCOM, LEH) do genuinely return zero rows -- but that is a ticker-mapping failure on the query side, not an SF1 coverage property. Of the two WorldCom-related strings tried, MCIP (MCI Inc, the post-scandal renamed entity) DOES exist in SF1 but was filtered out of the R18 panel for having too few consecutive quarterly observations -- a panel-construction exclusion, not an absence.
2. Wider 18-name set: 18/18 present in SF1, 18/18 of those also in the R18 analysis panel. Zero genuine absences found in either list.
3. No era or exchange skew to report -- there is nothing missing to skew.
4. WIKI/PRICES covers none of the tested names (including the historically correct, pre-collapse tickers) -- a separate, genuine gap in that free dataset, uninformative about SF1's own coverage.

CONCLUSION: Section 3.1's 'catastrophic delistings are absent from our SF1 pull' framing is WRONG as stated and should be corrected, not merely narrowed. The honest statement is the reverse of what's currently written: every catastrophic delisting checked (21 of 21 named companies, headline three plus the wider set) is present in SF1 and already contributing to the R18 panel and the paper's own estimates; the only real finding is that the specific ticker strings originally queried for the three headline names were wrong.
