FINDING: the gap between last filing and actual delisting IS systematically
LONGER for high-DeltaS firms. R18's filing-date-spaced construction therefore
still conditions on survival in the direction the paper is correcting for --
high-disorder firms that stop filing early are removed from the panel BEFORE
their true delisting date, understating how long the panel 'sees' distress in
exactly the firms whose absence would matter most for the entropy premium.
Section 3.1 should state this explicitly. The direction is conservative for the
headline collapse (it would, if anything, make the corrected panel's ΔS premium
MORE attenuated toward the survivorship-biased result, not less -- so it does not
undermine the '+4.70 -> +0.02' collapse itself) but the manuscript should not
describe R18 as a full listing-survivorship correction without this caveat.
