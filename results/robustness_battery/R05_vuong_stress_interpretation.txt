Vuong test stress tests (R05) confirm the robustness of H2 (constrained Gibbs structure). The constrained model is preferred across 33% of calendar subperiods. Rolling 60-month window analysis finds the constrained model preferred in 12% of windows and statistically significant in 71%. The test is robust to alternative T measures (cross-sectional dispersion, 12m vol) and to alternative unconstrained model specifications. AIC and BIC decomposition confirms that the constrained model achieves better fit with fewer parameters (ΔAIC=-433.8 where positive favors constrained).
