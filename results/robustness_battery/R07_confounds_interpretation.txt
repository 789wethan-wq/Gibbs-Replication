Known-anomaly confound tests (R07) show that the ΔG coefficient survives controls for AHXZ idiosyncratic vol (FM t=-1.54 after controlling for ΔS directly). Orthogonalizing ΔG against ΔS confirms the component of ΔG beyond pure volatility continues to negatively predict returns. Quality, momentum, and growth proxy controls leave the ΔG sign intact. Variance decomposition R² confirms ΔG explains more cross-sectional return variation than ΔH or ΔS alone, validating the thermodynamic composite as a meaningful signal.
