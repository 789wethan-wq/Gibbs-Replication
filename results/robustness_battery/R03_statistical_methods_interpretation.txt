Statistical methodology tests confirm that the core FM t-statistic for ΔG is robust to the choice of Newey-West lags (range: [-4.16, -3.95] across lags 3–18), to winsorization intensity (0.5%–5%), to OLS vs. rank-based regressions, and to generalized least squares weighting. The logistic direction-of-effect test confirms that low-ΔG predicts higher probability of landing in the top return quintile.
