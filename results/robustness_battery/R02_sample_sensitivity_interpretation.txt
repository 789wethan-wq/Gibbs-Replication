Across all sample and time-period robustness tests in Category R02, the sign inversion (Q5−Q1 negative) holds in 100% of specifications and the FM t-statistic exceeds 2.5 in absolute value in 33% of specifications. Rolling 60-month window analysis shows the signal is negative in 100% of windows and statistically significant (|t|>2.0) in 33%, confirming the result is not concentrated in a single calendar period. Crisis exclusion tests confirm the sign inversion persists when dot-com, GFC, and COVID windows are removed, ruling out a crisis-only artifact.
