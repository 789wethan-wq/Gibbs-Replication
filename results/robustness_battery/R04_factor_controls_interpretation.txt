Factor control robustness (R04) confirms that the negative ΔG premium survives across CAPM, FF3, FF5, and FF5+UMD factor model controls, with negative L/S alpha in 100% of factor model specifications. Double-sorting within volatility (size proxy) quintiles and adding momentum/reversal controls to Fama-MacBeth regressions leaves the ΔG coefficient negative and statistically significant, suggesting the result is not a pure size, momentum, or beta effect.
