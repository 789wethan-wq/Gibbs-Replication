Regime sensitivity tests (R06) show that the ΔG premium is stronger in high-temperature (disordered market) regimes, consistent with the thermodynamic non-equilibrium hypothesis. FM coefficients remain negative across both Markov-switching and threshold-based regime identification methods. T window sensitivity analysis finds the sign inversion robust across 12–60 month temperature measures (t-stat range: [-3.99, -3.97]). The T×ΔG interaction term is significant in the FM specification that includes it, confirming the Gibbs temperature weighting is not incidental.
