Microstructure robustness tests (R08) confirm that the L/S return sign inversion is robust to transaction cost haircuts, with the negative sign surviving in 100% of TC scenarios (net monthly return at 30bps cost: -0.0179). Skip-month (t+2) analysis confirms the result is not driven by bid-ask bounce. Holding period analysis shows the signal strengthens at longer horizons (3–12m), consistent with a slow-moving fundamental effect rather than microstructure noise. Restricting to the more-liquid, lower-volatility half of the universe preserves the sign inversion, suggesting the result is implementable.
